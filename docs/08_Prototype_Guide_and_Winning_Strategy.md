## 2. Live Demo Script (Step-by-Step for Hackathon Jury)

1. **Step 1: Open Application Workspace**
   - Direct jury to [https://prudence-ai.vercel.app](https://prudence-ai.vercel.app).
   - Show the 3D wireframe architectural canvas on the hero section with space-grotesk headlines.

2. **Step 2: Launch Engine & Load Sample Drawing**
   - Click **`Scan Blueprint Now`** (or select sample file `Green_Heights_Block_A.dwg`).
   - Point out that the drawing automatically scales to **100% viewport height** with zero page scrolling.

3. **Step 3: Demonstrate Visual Pin Callouts**
   - Highlight Pin `01` (Rear Setback Deficit) and Pin `02` (Fire Evacuation Staircase Width).
   - Show how leader lines connect directly to exact spatial coordinates on the drawing canvas.

4. **Step 4: Demonstrate Accordion Statutory Sidebar**
   - Click to expand Pin `01` card on the right rail.
   - Show measurement comparison grid ($2.40\text{m}$ provided vs $4.00\text{m}$ required) and recommended architectural remediation.

5. **Step 5: Ask Grounded AI Assistant**
   - Open the Groq AI Chat drawer.
   - Type *"How do I fix the rear setback violation on Sheet 1?"*
   - Show the instant markdown answer generated by Groq `openai/gpt-oss-120b`.
